Two months since anyone touched it. A competitive read of the live-music app space, a fresh review of every screen, and the first real walk through the app on a phone-sized screen with a test account. Fixes were shipped as they were found; features are ranked below for you to choose from.

The one thing to take from this. The capture-from-a-ticket pitch is no longer unique — Gigs (iOS 26, April 2026) does it on-device for free, and Google Wallet does it natively on Android. What nobody else has is the layer after capture: flights and stays clustered into trips, a private life map with a real privacy model, a cross-ticketer friends graph, and one deploy to web, iOS and Android. Lead with that. And the social tier at the centre of it was, until today, impossible to join.

Threats worth knowing about

  • Rotating barcodes. Ticketmaster SafeTix rotates every 15 s, AXS every ~60 s, DICE withholds the QR until near doors. A captured barcode will not scan at the door for most UK arena and club tickets. If night mode keeps showing it as if it will, the first review says "turned away".
  • Gigs has the same pitch, on-device, free, from a known indie developer, with setlist import and a Wrapped card already shipped. iOS-only, no travel, no social, no Android.
  • Single-source feed. Ticketmaster's API terms could change after the April 2026 antitrust verdict.
  • Songkick is now owned by Suno, and users were told their data moved to an AI-music company. A "never trained on" pledge plus a Songkick import is a cheap, pointed differentiator.
  • Reliability is the review-killer across this category: duplicates, lost gigs, un-editable fields. One data-loss report in a 13-rating app is fatal.

What to build next — ranked by impact per effort

Effort is for one developer working with an assistant. "Native build" means the iOS and Android shells must be rebuilt; a web deploy is not enough.

1

Issuer-aware night mode

Have the parser classify the issuer and entry mode (static barcode / rotating in-app / app-locked). Night mode then shows the captured barcode only for static issuers, and a one-tap deep link into the Ticketmaster, AXS or DICE app for the rest — "Open in DICE, your QR appears there". Store the order number for the box office.

Days · additive migration · no native build · turns the biggest threat into a feature

2

Shareable Wrapped card, before early December

Enable the disabled Share button on year-in-review: a 1080×1920 story card from stats the page already computes, through the PNG pipeline that /api/countdown already has. Bandsintown emails its recap mid-December; fans built an unofficial RA Wrapped app because demand is that strong.

Days · no migration · no native build · the January growth loop

3

Setlist on every past gig

Morning after a gig, query setlist.fm by artist + date + city, attach it to the pin, show it on the item page and in memories, offer a one-tap "open as a playlist" search. The single most-wanted enrichment in competitor reviews; GigTrotter has none.

Days · additive migration · free API, key already scaffolded

4

History import: setlist.fm + CSV

Turn the first two "Coming soon" cards on Import into real ones. The serious gig-goer's incumbent is a spreadsheet, and an empty map on day one is the cold-start killer.

Days · no migration if imports write ordinary wallet items

5

Spotify connect at onboarding

Finish the OAuth scaffold: pull top artists, auto-follow with a review list, and the next nightly Bandsintown pass fills "For you". Every competitor's step one. Today the app has zero follows in production.

Days · needs SPOTIFY_CLIENT_ID/SECRET

6

Push notifications

Alerts that only appear when you open the app are not alerts. The four kinds already exist and dedupe; this needs a device-token table, a send route, APNs/FCM, and a native build. It is also the cleanest answer to Apple's "just a website" guideline.

Weeks · migration · native build

7

Fix sign-in inside the shells

Google OAuth in an embedded WebView is blocked by Google; Apple requires Sign in with Apple alongside any social login. Until both are done, email + password is the only route that works on a phone.

Days · native build · unverified but very likely

8

"Can't go?" + want-a-ticket waitlist

One button on every ticket that routes by issuer (DICE Return, Ticketmaster Transfer, give to a friend). On the other side, "Want one" on sold-out feed events, notifying inner circle first when a spare appears. DICE's waiting list is its most-praised feature.

Days · additive migration · builds on item 1

9

Anniversary and "you were both there" alerts

Promote the On-this-day card to an alert kind; surface shared past gigs on the item page. Pure additions to the alert generator.

Hours

10

Skiddle as a third feed source

The grassroots and club tier Ticketmaster under-indexes, with an affiliate scheme. One more mapper against the existing contract.

Days · ranked low because discovery is hygiene, not the reason to install

Smaller improvements from the review (hours each)

  • Staged progress during the 5–15 s parse, with a realistic time expectation.
  • Show the screenshot on the confirm card — the encrypted blob is write-only today, so a 20-card backfill queue is 20 identical-looking forms.
  • "Confirm all ≥ 80%" for backfill.
  • Make the globe usable on a phone: one-finger drag rotates it instead of scrolling the page.
  • Notify on friend request and acceptance — today a request is only visible if the other person opens People.
  • A Share button on the public board; a link preview for /b/<handle>.
  • Browse events is 30-and-done: add load-more and a date filter.
  • "Going" interest and "Add to wallet" are two disconnected ways to say the same thing; Wishlist and Follows likewise.
  • Swipe-back in the iOS shell (one line of Capacitor config, native build).
  • Reconsider the five bottom tabs: "Tonight" is empty 29 days a month; Events and Alerts are buried.
  • A minimal offline story for gig night: a global online/offline banner at least.

Do not build

  • A recommendation engine or any attempt to out-discover Spotify, Apple Music or Bandsintown. Seed follows from Spotify and stop.
  • Reconstructing rotating barcodes. Impossible without the issuer's secret; deep-link instead.
  • Unhiding the marketplace for screenshot-listed tickets. You cannot prove a ticket is real or unique, and the draft UK law makes the platform liable with fines up to 10% of turnover. If it ever opens: issuer-transfer flows only, Stripe escrow, a written guarantee.
  • The merch store and perks club now. DICE sells merch inside its own checkout and GigTrotter has no supply or audience yet.
  • Venue-ops tooling. Opendate and GigPig own it; the venue angle is audience-side only.
  • Streaks or compulsory gamification. Afterlight sells "no streaks" as a feature.
  • A $9.99 Plus tier or gating social behind it. The market clears at £2.99–3.99 a month, gating storage, exports, imports and deep stats only.
  • A native rewrite. One deploy to three platforms is the one thing the ten iOS-only diary apps cannot match.
